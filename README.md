# Hi there, I am Banashree 👋 (she/her)

I am a **Theoretical Physics Postdoc in Chennai Mathematical Institute (CMI)** working in **String Field Theory** and **Quantum Metrology**, holding a **PhD in Quantum Chaos**. My work focuses on navigating the boundaries between highly chaotic quantum systems, fundamental string dynamics, and precision quantum measurement frameworks.

---

## 👀 Research & Interests
* **Quantum Chaos:** Involving computation of Out-of-time-ordered correlators (OTOC) in strongly coupled quantum systems
* **String Field Theory:** Developing algebraic and computational tools to handle string interactions.
* **Quantum Metrology:** Designing optimised quantum states and protocols for high-precision parameter estimation.
* **Computational Physics:** Translating abstract, high-level mathematical frameworks into clean numerical algorithms.
